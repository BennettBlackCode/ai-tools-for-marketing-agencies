## How we picked

- **One job each.** Every tool here is the best pick for a specific agency job, not a general "AI writer."
- **Agency fit.** Works across many clients, with white label or reporting, and pricing that makes sense at 5 or 50 clients.
- **Works with AI agents.** Extra credit for an API or MCP server, so ChatGPT or Claude can run the tool for you.
- **Real output.** It has to produce client work (a site, a lead list, a report), not just ideas.

## AI assistants

### 1. ChatGPT: best all-around marketing AI

[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) is the default starting point for most agency work: drafting copy, summarizing calls, analyzing spreadsheets and researching a client's market.

- Deep research for market and competitor briefs
- Custom GPTs for repeatable client tasks
- Apps and connectors to pull in your other tools

**Pricing:** Free plan, paid plans for individuals and teams.

### 2. Claude: best for long-form writing and connected workflows

[Claude](https://claude.ai) from Anthropic is strong at long, careful writing and analysis. Its connectors (MCP servers) let it use your other tools directly, so one chat can pull leads, build a site and draft the outreach.

- Projects keep each client's docs and brand rules in one place
- Connectors for tools like LocalProspects, 1ClickWebsite and DataForSEO
- Claude Code automates repeatable tasks for technical teams

**Pricing:** Free plan, paid plans for individuals and teams.

## Websites

### 3. 1ClickWebsite: best AI website builder for agencies

[1ClickWebsite](https://www.1clickwebsite.ai) turns a two-minute form into a complete WordPress site with copy, images, and SEO service and location pages, in under 5 minutes. It's built for agencies selling websites to local businesses.

- Full WordPress sites with WP Admin access, mobile optimized
- Meta titles and descriptions on every page
- Lead gen: upload a CSV of prospects and get a personalized demo site for each one
- Website Agent: edit finished sites by chatting with AI
- 100% white label, with a client portal on the Agency plan
- API and MCP server, so Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor can create and edit sites

**Pricing:** Starter $49/mo (3 sites), Professional $97/mo (10 sites), Agency $297/mo (35 sites, white label), Unlimited $1,297/mo.

## Lead generation

### 4. LocalProspects: best for local business leads

[LocalProspects](https://localprospects.ai) pulls every business for a niche and city from live Google Maps data. It then adds the owner's name, business emails, phone line type (mobile, landline or VoIP) and social profiles to each lead.

- Search any niche in any city, or a whole state at once
- Owner names and ranked business emails on every lead, at no extra cost
- Phone line type with carrier, so you know which numbers can get a text
- CSV export, REST API and an MCP server for AI agents

**Pricing:** Free (100 leads), Starter $39/mo, Agency $97/mo, Scale $297/mo.

## SEO and AI search

### 5. LLM Sleuth: best for AI search visibility (AEO)

Clients now ask "do we show up in ChatGPT?" [LLM Sleuth](https://llmsleuth.com) answers that. It runs one question through ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity side by side, then shows what each model searched, what it answered and which pages it cited.

- See which brands each AI names, and which it leaves out
- The exact searches the models ran to find their sources
- Headings, word count and schema of every cited page, to use as a content brief
- Monthly re-runs to track progress, PDF export and an API

**Pricing:** Free trial (4 credits), Starter $29/mo, Pro $49/mo, Agency $129/mo.

### 6. Semrush: best for SEO and competitor research

[Semrush](https://www.semrush.com) is the all-in-one SEO suite most agencies already know: keyword research, competitor traffic, site audits and rank tracking, with client-ready reports.

- Find the keywords a client's competitors rank for
- Technical site audits and position tracking
- Client reports you can brand as your own

**Pricing:** Free limited account, paid plans per user.

### 7. DataForSEO: best SEO data API

[DataForSEO](https://dataforseo.com) sells raw SEO data by API: SERPs, keyword volumes, backlinks, on-page audits, business listings, reviews and AI mentions. Agencies use it to build their own rank trackers and reports instead of paying per seat.

- Pay only for the data you pull, no subscription
- Keyword, SERP, backlink and AI visibility data in one account
- MCP server, so AI agents can pull the data directly

**Pricing:** Pay as you go, $50 minimum top-up, $1 free trial credit.

## Content and creative

### 8. Jasper: best for on-brand copy at scale

[Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai) is an AI marketing platform built around brand voice. You load each client's style and facts, then generate campaign copy that stays on brand.

- Brand voice and knowledge per client
- Campaign workflows for ads, emails and landing pages
- Team approvals and collaboration

**Pricing:** Paid plans, free trial.

### 9. Canva: best for fast on-brand design

[Canva](https://www.canva.com) with Magic Studio covers most day-to-day client design: social posts, ads, decks and one-pagers, with AI to generate and resize.

- Brand kits per client
- AI image generation, editing and instant resizing
- Easy client sharing and approvals

**Pricing:** Free plan, paid Pro and Teams plans.

### 10. Descript: best for video and podcast editing

[Descript](https://www.descript.com) lets you edit video by editing its transcript. Agencies use it to cut client videos, podcasts and short clips fast.

- Delete words in the transcript to cut the video
- Remove filler words and clean up audio
- Turn long videos into short social clips

**Pricing:** Free plan, paid plans.

## Operations and automation

### 11. HubSpot: best CRM with built-in AI

[HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com) runs the CRM, email and marketing ops for many agencies and their clients. Its Breeze AI features add drafting, research and agents inside the CRM.

- Free CRM to start
- AI help writing emails, landing pages and reports
- Deep marketing automation on paid hubs

**Pricing:** Free CRM, paid hubs.

### 12. Zapier: best for connecting your tools

[Zapier](https://zapier.com) connects thousands of apps, so a new lead, form fill or signed contract can kick off the next step automatically.

- No-code workflows across your whole stack
- AI steps inside workflows
- Easy handoffs between sales, delivery and reporting

**Pricing:** Free plan, paid plans by task volume.

### 13. Grok Bot: best for hands-off campaign setup

[Grok Bot](https://x.ai/bot) from xAI is an AI teammate that works inside your tools (Google Docs, Google Ads, a browser and your site's code) rather than just chatting.

- Researches competitors and writes a strategic summary
- Drafts positioning, landing page outlines and ad copy in Google Docs
- Builds Google Ads campaign structures and recommends what to scale or cut

**Pricing:** Free to start, paid plans available.

## The best AI stack by agency type

**Web design agency:** LocalProspects (find local businesses) → 1ClickWebsite (send each one a demo site) → Claude (write the outreach) → Canva (social and ads).

**SEO agency:** Semrush or DataForSEO (research and tracking) → LLM Sleuth (AI search visibility) → Claude (content briefs and drafts) → 1ClickWebsite (service and location pages).

**Paid social and ads agency:** Grok Bot (campaign research and setup) → Jasper (on-brand copy) → Canva (creative) → Descript (video).

**Small or solo agency:** ChatGPT or Claude, plus Zapier to connect everything. Add one tool per service you sell.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI tool for marketing agencies?
There isn't one. ChatGPT or Claude covers general drafting and research. The real gains come from a tool per job: 1ClickWebsite for client websites, LocalProspects for local leads, Semrush for SEO and LLM Sleuth for AI search visibility.

### What AI tools do web design agencies use?
Most use an AI website builder like 1ClickWebsite to build client sites in minutes, a lead tool like LocalProspects to find local businesses, and ChatGPT or Claude to write outreach and copy.

### How do I check if a client shows up in ChatGPT or Perplexity?
Use an AI visibility tool like LLM Sleuth. It runs your question through ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, and shows whether the client is named and which pages the models cited instead.

### Are there free AI tools for marketing agencies?
Yes. ChatGPT, Claude, Canva, Zapier, Descript and the HubSpot CRM all have free plans. LocalProspects includes 100 free leads, and LLM Sleuth has a free trial.

### Which AI tools work with ChatGPT and Claude?
Tools with an API or MCP server can be run by AI agents directly. On this list that includes 1ClickWebsite, LocalProspects, LLM Sleuth and DataForSEO.
