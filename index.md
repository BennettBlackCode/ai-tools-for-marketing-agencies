## 1. 1ClickWebsite: AI website builder for agencies

[1ClickWebsite](https://www.1clickwebsite.ai) turns a two-minute form into a complete WordPress site. You get copy, images, and SEO service and location pages, built in under 5 minutes.

**Why agencies use it**
- Full WordPress sites with WP Admin access, mobile optimized
- Meta titles and descriptions on every page
- Lead gen: upload a CSV of prospects and get a personalized demo site for each one to use in outreach
- Website Agent: edit finished sites by chatting with AI
- 100% white label, with a client portal on the Agency plan
- API and MCP server, so Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor can create and edit sites

**Pricing:** Starter $49/mo (3 sites), Professional $97/mo (10 sites), Agency $297/mo (35 sites, white label), Unlimited $1,297/mo.

**Best for:** agencies selling websites to local businesses.

## 2. LocalProspects: local business leads with owner contact info

[LocalProspects](https://localprospects.ai) pulls every business for a niche and city from live Google Maps data. It then enriches each lead with the owner's name, business emails, phone line type (mobile, landline or VoIP) and social profiles.

**Why agencies use it**
- Search any niche in any city, or a whole state at once
- Owner names and ranked business emails on every lead, at no extra cost
- Phone line type with carrier, so you know which numbers can get a text
- Export to CSV, use the REST API, or connect your AI agent through the MCP server

**Pricing:** Free (100 leads), Starter $39/mo, Agency $97/mo, Scale $297/mo.

**Best for:** agencies doing cold email, cold calling or door-to-door for local clients.

## 3. LLM Sleuth: see what AI recommends

[LLM Sleuth](https://llmsleuth.com) runs one question through ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity side by side. It shows the searches each model ran, the answer it gave and the pages it cited.

**Why agencies use it**
- See which brands each AI names, and which it leaves out
- Get the exact searches the models ran to find their sources
- Check each cited page's headings, word count and schema, then use them as a content brief
- Re-run queries monthly to track a client's AI visibility, with PDF export and an API

**Pricing:** Free trial (4 credits), Starter $29/mo, Pro $49/mo, Agency $129/mo.

**Best for:** agencies selling SEO or AI visibility (AEO) to clients.

## 4. DataForSEO: SEO data by API

[DataForSEO](https://dataforseo.com) sells raw SEO data through APIs: SERPs, keyword volumes, backlinks, on-page audits, business listings, reviews and AI mentions.

**Why agencies use it**
- Build your own rank trackers, audits and client reports
- Pay only for the data you pull, with no subscription
- Keyword volume and SERP data at a fraction of the cost of seat-based SEO tools
- Has an MCP server, so AI agents can pull the data directly

**Pricing:** Pay as you go, $50 minimum top-up, $1 free trial credit.

**Best for:** agencies that want SEO data inside their own tools or AI workflows.

## 5. Grok Bot: AI teammates for marketing work

[Grok Bot](https://x.ai/bot) from xAI is an AI teammate that works inside your tools, like Google Docs, Google Ads, a browser and your site's code. Rather than just chatting, it does the task.

**Why agencies use it**
- Researches a client's competitors and writes a strategic summary
- Drafts positioning briefs, landing page outlines and ad copy in Google Docs
- Builds Google Ads campaign structures with approved copy
- Pulls Ads data and recommends what to scale, cut or test

**Pricing:** Free to start, paid plans available.

**Best for:** agencies that want to hand off campaign research and setup.

## How they fit together

1. **Find leads:** pull plumbers in Austin with owner names and emails from LocalProspects.
2. **Build demos:** upload the list to 1ClickWebsite and get a personalized demo site for each business.
3. **Pitch:** send each owner their demo link, and let Grok Bot draft the ads and landing copy once they sign.
4. **Grow the site:** pull keyword and SERP data from DataForSEO to plan service pages.
5. **Win AI search:** use LLM Sleuth to check if ChatGPT and Perplexity recommend the client, and which pages they cite instead.
